Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about our clinic, appointments, and billing.

Is there a free consultation available?

Yes, we offer a free 15-minute initial tele-consultation for new patients to discuss their symptoms and determine the right specialist for their needs.

Can I change or cancel my appointment later?

Absolutely. You can reschedule or cancel your appointment up to 24 hours in advance without any penalty through our online patient portal or by calling our support desk.

What is your cancellation and refund policy?

Cancellations made within 24 hours of the scheduled time may be subject to a nominal cancellation fee. Refunds for advance payments are processed within 3-5 business days to the original payment method.

Can other info be added to a medical invoice?

Yes. If you need specific details such as your company name, tax ID, or insurance policy number added to your invoice, please inform our billing department before your final checkout.

How does insurance billing work?

We offer direct billing to most major insurance providers. You only need to pay the co-pay amount or deductibles at the time of your visit. Our team handles the claim submission for you.

Quick Answers

Explore Our Knowledge Base

Hover over the cards below to instantly reveal the answers to our most common questions.

Do I need a referral to see a specialist?

In most cases, you don't need a referral to book an appointment with our specialists. However, certain insurance plans may require one for claim approvals.

Do you accept international insurance?

Yes, we partner with major global insurance providers. Our billing team will assist you in direct billing or provide the necessary documents for reimbursement.

Is telehealth consultation available?

Absolutely! We offer secure 24/7 video consultations with our doctors. You can easily book a virtual slot through our online patient portal.

How long do laboratory test results take?

Standard blood and pathology test results are typically available within 24 to 48 hours. You will receive an SMS and email notification once they are ready.

Can I get my prescriptions refilled online?

Yes. Registered patients can request medication refills through our app. Our pharmacy also offers free city-wide delivery for approved refills.

Is the clinic fully wheelchair accessible?

Yes, our entire facility is designed to be fully accessible, including dedicated parking spots, ramps, and spacious elevators for your absolute comfort.
