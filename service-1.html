Prescription Drugs Dispensing (Required by law in all Manitoba pharmacies)

At Lakeshore gimli, we understand that managing your medications can be a complex and time-consuming process. That's why we offer convenient and reliable prescription dispensing services to ensure that you have access to your medications when you need them. Our dedicated team of pharmacists and technicians is committed to providing personalized care and exceptional service to every patient.

What is Prescription Dispensing?

Prescription dispensing involves the accurate and safe preparation of medications prescribed by your healthcare provider. Our pharmacy team works diligently to fill your prescriptions ensuring that you receive the right medications in the correct dosage and quantity.

How Prescription Dispensing Works at Lakeshore Medical Clinic

  • Prescription Submission : You can ask your healthcare professionals to fax us directly your prescription or you can bring it in-person. We accept prescriptions from all healthcare providers, including physicians, nurse practitioners, and dentists.
  • Prescription Review : Our pharmacists carefully review each prescription to ensure accuracy, appropriateness, and compatibility with your existing medications and medical history.
  • Medication Preparation : Once your prescription has been reviewed, our pharmacy team will accurately prepare your medications in accordance with your healthcare provider's instructions.
  • Patient Counseling : Before dispensing your medications, our pharmacists will provide counseling on how to take your medications properly, potential side effects to watch for, and any other relevant information.
